🌿 Cycle Phase Wellness & Hormonal Insights

Current: Menstrual Phase
Menstrual Phase (Days 1–5): Estrogen and progesterone are at their lowest levels. Your body is shedding the uterine lining and asks for gentle restoration and rest.

🥗 Nutrition & Cravings

Eat warm, iron-rich meals like lentils, leafy greens, bone broths, and dark chocolate. Drink ginger or chamomile tea to soothe uterine cramps.

🏃‍♀️ Movement & Exercise

Prioritize slow restorative yoga, gentle stretching, and mindful strolls. Give your body permission to rest without guilt.

💆‍♀️ Self-Care & Mood

Keep your lower abdomen warm with a heating pad, sleep 8+ hours, and create space for introspection and cozy quiet time.

🧬 Hormones & Energy

Lowest hormone levels mean lower physical energy but heightened intuition and emotional clarity. Honor your natural rhythms.
